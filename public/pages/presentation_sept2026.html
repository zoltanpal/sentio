What is Sentio?

A clear view of the mood behind the news.

Search a company, person, event or topic. Sentio brings together news sentiment, coverage and related stories so you can understand the conversation and explore the headlines behind it.

English news · Time windows from 6 hours to 3 days

Sentiment and coverage

Put the current news in context

Current tone

How does the coverage read?

The sentiment score and positive, neutral and negative shares summarise the tone of matching articles.

Trend

Is the tone shifting?

The comparison with an equivalent previous period shows whether sentiment is improving, declining or stable.

Coverage

How much attention is it getting?

Article and source counts show the breadth of coverage. Percentage changes reveal increases or decreases in attention.

Story activity

Which stories are gaining coverage?

“New” and “More coverage” labels highlight stories appearing in the selected period or receiving more articles.

Period comparisons depend on available history. The full 3d window has no previous-period comparison.
